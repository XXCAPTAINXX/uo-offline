# Solo progression update

Starter supplies are free, including earrings, the leveling cape (`[cape`) and evolving sash (`[sash`). Player equipment evolves only when it is designated special gear (starter/evolving gear and Haven/Astral rewards). All companion equipment remains eligible, including ordinary gear supplied by the player. Equipped eligible gear gains shared experience from credited monster kills and companion support/combat. Generic progression adds Luck and Strength/Dexterity/Intelligence; weapon, grimoire and cape progression remains specialized. Gear has a maximum level of 20. Consumables and utility containers do not level.

Arcane Supplies at bank service areas sells complete spellbooks, runebooks, a 48-rune atlas, sending supplies, durability powder, blessing deeds, reagents and ammunition. Training Supplies sells 105/110 power scrolls and a compatible custom mastery manual. Mastery requires 90 skill and applies one 30-minute stat focus; this is not the later expansion's ability system.

Companions defend their owner's current target or active nearby attackers while following/guarding, and preserve Stay orders. Fighter, Healer, Bard, Caster and Archer roles are available. Gear swaps retain old equipment. Resource regeneration and native spell targeting are included.

Healing and Veterinary bandage timers are capped at two seconds, including resurrection attempts. A Healing buff icon displays on the character applying bandages and clears on completion/cancellation. Companion support heals immediately, so it has no bandage countdown.

Wallet double-click collects nearby loose gold; `[wallet` opens balances and withdrawal. Say `withdraw 1000` or use `[withdraw 1000`. Ordinary merchants and upgrades can spend wallet gold. Astral Shards from eligible monster kills are stored in the wallet and buy special rewards.

Travel menus display twelve compact destinations per page. Shops show item stats on inspection and tooltips over entries.

Dead companions resurrect automatically after five seconds near their online owner. Refresh resurrects immediately when nearby, preserving their identity and gear.

Animal Taming and Animal Lore do not count against a player's existing total skill cap. They still require training and obey individual skill caps and locks. Natural gains and NPC training use the same exemption. Use [skillbudget to see the counted total; the standard client skill total still includes all skills.

Training Supplies now opens Combat, Magic, Bards/Tamers, Crafting, and Pet Supplies categories. Shop entries show short inline bonuses plus full single-argument hover text, avoiding client newline truncation.

Pet supplies include six-scroll 105 bundles (15,000 gold), six-scroll 110 bundles (45,000 gold), bonding potions (2,500), reusable shrinking leashes (5,000), and house shrinking posts (10,000). All use wallet payments. Bundles contain Wrestling, Tactics, Anatomy, Magic Resistance, Meditation and Focus; Magery and Evaluating Intelligence are excluded. Scrolls apply only to nearby, living, owned pets and raise caps without granting skill points or abilities. Invalid or redundant uses preserve the scroll.

House posts must be placed in a house the user owns or co-owns and can be locked down. Leashes reuse the town shrinking rules. Bonding potions consume only after successfully bonding an eligible owned pet. The new Arcane and Training stones move to 3504,2583 and 3508,2583 around the south side of the monument, clearing their former east-side locations.

Companion base Strength, Dexterity and Intelligence gain two points per training level beyond level one. Helpful actions contribute training as well as gear experience, with a ten-second cooldown. Offline training counts too. Growth never lowers existing base stats, stacks again on refresh, or refills current resources. The Stats tab shows base stats separately.

Companion direct healing, cures and resurrection support reach 12 tiles with line of sight. Gear/pack interactions retain their short range.

Haven Island now grants +1,000 Luck to players on Trammel, including New Haven and Old Haven. It stacks with equipment luck and is computed from location, so it cannot accumulate across movement or reloads. Outside the island it stops applying. Use [havenluck to see total and area bonus.

Player skills train faster below 100 on Haven Island: five times normal gain chance and five times normal gain amount, stopping the larger increment at 100. Skill checks still need valid practice; individual caps, skill locks and the existing counted total cap remain enforced. Above 100 and outside Haven, normal training applies.

The player total skill cap is now 1,000 points and applies automatically on login for new and existing characters. Animal Taming and Animal Lore remain excluded from this budget. Companion caps are unchanged.

Wallets now store Haven marks with a separate balance. Double-clicking collects backpack marks along with gold, retaining nearby gold pickup. [wallet opens Haven rewards and supports mark withdrawals. Bracelets and robe upgrades spend stored marks first, then loose marks, and only use gold when there are not enough total marks. Wallet v0/v1 saves migrate without losing gold or Astral shards.

The champion pendant costs 250 Haven marks, with no gold alternative. Its base bonuses include 1,000 Luck, +20 Str/Dex/Int, +5 hit/stamina/mana regeneration, 40% weapon and spell damage, 15% hit/defense chance, 10% swing speed, +1 faster casting/+3 cast recovery, 10% lower mana cost and 100% lower reagent cost. Existing bracelets are unchanged.

Bards learn Musicianship, Discordance, Peacemaking and Provocation. Songs unlock at 80 Music/Peace, show timed buff icons, last 60 seconds and refresh every 45 seconds. At 90 Music, they adapt their stat emphasis to health, mana and combat style. Hostile bard attempts are spaced by 20 seconds: emergency peacemaking, provocation between hostile attackers, or Discordance. Role changes/death/deletion clear maintained song buffs.

Astral shards now arrive as physical backpack items even when carrying a wallet. Double-click shards to deposit them. If the backpack is full, drops appear at the player's feet.

Bard companions offer Tame assist from Orders. Select a nearby wild tamable animal, then tame it normally. The companion stays in support mode, attempts native Peacemaking at most every 12 seconds while the target is not pacified, and continues normal healing. It does not attack while assisting. Stop assist ends the mode; successful taming, moving away, death or changing roles also ends it. Peacemaking success depends on bard skills and the animal's difficulty; companions do not tame or claim pets themselves.

Haven housing: players may place houses on suitable clear land in New Haven and the island. Normal terrain, road, obstruction and neighboring-house checks still apply. Shops, the service square, moongate and Warden encounter area remain protected. Other towns retain their housing rules.

The Warden drops 3–6 Haven marks per kill and its spawner checks every 2–3 minutes. Vampiric steed spawners check every 30–60 seconds, still limited to one wild spawn per spawner. Existing saved spawners receive these timers at startup without replacing their living creatures or tamed pets.

Matching Haven sets: nine themed rings cost 30 Haven marks each, copying their bracelet's base bonuses and gaining shared gear experience. Wearing the corresponding bracelet activates the set bonus described in the ring's tooltip and shop preview. Bonuses are computed from equipped pieces, so removing/swapping gear cannot leave permanent stacked bonuses.

The Haven talisman of concord costs 150 marks, grants 300 Luck, +10 all stats, +3 all regens, +20% weapon/spell damage, 5% lower mana cost and 20% lower reagent cost, and evolves with shared experience. With a matching ring/bracelet pair it adds +250 Luck and +10% weapon/spell damage. Companions can equip it through Gear.

Companion weapon evolution adds Hit Energy Area at level 5/10/15/20 with 10/20/30/40% chances, preserving stronger existing area attributes. At level 20, the native slayer bonus applies to all eligible wild monsters while the weapon is wielded by a companion; it does not stack multiple slayer multipliers. Owner/player/pet protections and Tame assist still suppress harmful actions. Existing weapon levels qualify on the next shared experience gain.

The Warden now also drops two guaranteed random equipment pieces with 3–5 magic properties at 50–85 intensity (native luck rolls apply), 1,500–2,500 gold plus a Rich loot pack, a 20% chance of a matching-set ring and a separate 15% bracelet chance. Marks remain 3–6 and respawn timing remains 2–3 minutes. Rewards are generated at death, avoiding duplicate custom payouts at spawn.

The wallet uses a tinted gold-coin icon (0xEEF, hue 0x8A5) instead of the pouch. Existing wallets receive the appearance on deserialization without changing any balances. The Warden and its existing spawner are relocated from the enclosed ruin at 3670,2587 to open ground at 3698,2595, with a six-tile home range. Tests verify walking routes from the east and south approaches. Housing protection follows the new encounter site.

The repair bench now offers Repair ALL and Restore ALL with no targeting cursor. It processes equipped gear plus nested backpack contents, charges only eligible items (starter equipment free), and validates the entire bill before changing gear or spending money. Repair preserves maximum durability; Restore raises lost maximums to the item's normal type maximum and fully repairs those items.

Companion roles use appropriate evolving arms: a blade for melee, bow for archery, and spellbook for casting. Gear has a Claim evolving arms button for replacements. Physical weapon evolution reaches 40% damage increase, 20% swing speed, and 40% hit lower attack/defense, life leech, mana leech and energy area at level 20, preserving stronger existing rolls. Spellbooks instead reach 40% spell damage, 2 faster casting, 4 faster cast recovery, 10% lower mana cost, 100% lower reagent cost, 4 mana regeneration and 20 bonus mana. Native aggregate caps still apply.

Companion equipment also provides balanced role-independent bonuses as it levels: each level-20 piece grants +8% weapon/spell damage and +2 regeneration, hit/defense chance and lower mana cost while equipped by a companion. Weapons omit spell damage/lower mana cost; spellbooks omit weapon damage/hit chance. Switching roles preserves progress.

Use `[c` or `[companion`, then Tasks, to send a companion on a five-minute offscreen expedition. Grind returns gold, Haven marks and magic gear; gathering offers ore, wood, leather or reagents. Each completed minute grants additional training, +1 raw Strength/Dexterity/Intelligence and 10 equipped-gear experience. Return now gives rewards only for full minutes completed. Trips survive saves/restarts and return when the owner is online; they do not repeat automatically.

The stationary Haven training sentinel at Trammel (3564, 2585), east of New Haven, has 30,000 HP, cannot harm anyone, and refills instead of dying. It provides a reusable combat/spell training target with no kill loot. Bard song stat bonuses already scale with Musicianship, Peacemaking and accumulated training time; the buff icon shows the active song's bonuses.
Focus is also a free skill alongside Animal Taming and Animal Lore: it does not consume the 1,000-point budget. Individual skill caps still apply. Use [skillbudget to see the counted total.

Tasks > Taming missions offers pack horse (11.1), horse/forest ostard/giant beetle (29.1), dragon (93.9) and white wyrm (96.3). Both companion base Taming and Lore must meet the listed value. A full five-minute mission returns an owner-bound pet claim in the shared pack; move it to your backpack and double-click with enough follower slots. Early returns award training but no pet. Native tame skill/stat reductions apply, pets are not automatically bonded, and the player's skills determine obedience.

Custom-pet taming mission rarity: completing the full five minutes always returns the selected species, rolled as Common 70%, Rare 22%, Epic 7%, Legendary 1%. The skill gate determines whether the companion can pursue that species; there is no additional taming-failure roll. Rarity is stored in the claim and cannot be rerolled by saving or trying to claim with full follower slots. Rare/Epic/Legendary add 10/20/30% raw stats and HP, plus 5/10/15 skill points in existing combat/magic skills with minimum caps of 105/110/120. Rare adds a fire strike, Epic combat healing, Legendary energy damage and owner mana support; each uses a 12-second cooldown and only affects eligible wild enemies while controlled.

Custom species: Emberwing ostard requires 65 Taming/Lore, has fire strikes and 100 combat skill caps; Moonfang wolf requires 95, has combat healing and 110 caps; Stormscale drake requires 110, has energy/mana support and 120 caps. Their native taming reductions apply before mission rarity bonuses. Wild specimens also spawn on Haven Island near (3455,2630), (3600,2670), and (3740,2440), respectively, with 30–60, 60–120 and 120–240 minute respawn delays. Their custom abilities activate only while tamed. Tasks > Taming missions > Rare custom pets opens their mission choices.
Companions check their equipped arms every two seconds and restore their last melee weapon, ranged weapon or spellbook without needing a role change. Re-equipping respects temporary disarm locks and reuses gear in the shared pack.
The former champion progression archive is now Champion's Codex. Double-click collects compatible backpack items and opens an 18-row skill ledger with 105/110/115/120, Alacrity and Transcendence columns. Arrows withdraw original scrolls, Owned skills filters empty rows, and Other treasures lists marks/skulls/stat scrolls and other compatible items. Open bag keeps direct inventory access available.
Three additional custom mounts are available in taming missions and the wild: Frostmane steed (70 Taming/Lore, cold strike), Verdant llama (80, healing), Stormhorn kirin (105, energy strike/mana support). All allow male and female riders. Wild locations are near (3490,2640), (3500,2650), and (3515,2645) on Trammel Haven Island.

Rarity rolls and bonuses apply only to custom pets. Standard-species mission claims now have no rarity bonus, even if an older claim recorded a tier. Rarity benefits are shown on custom claims; custom species' innate ability labels no longer claim a rarity tier.


Use Animal Lore on your pet ticket while it is in your backpack to inspect the exact pet before claiming it. The ticket reserves the pet with native taming reductions and applicable custom rarity bonuses already applied, preserves it across saves and failed claims, and releases that same pet on successful redemption. Deleting an unused ticket also deletes its reserved pet.

Animal Training now uses slot stages rather than a separate 20-level system. Use Animal Lore on your owned pet and select Animal Training, or use [pettrain. Begin a stage, reach 100% combat progress, then spend weighted points on stats, resists, skill caps, or the learned Healing ability. The first purchase increases the pet's follower-slot cost by one and requires room in your follower capacity. Finish stage asks for confirmation before discarding leftover points. Existing stats above training limits are preserved, but cannot be raised further through training.

This is a Mondain's Legacy-compatible implementation of the Animal Training progression model, not a complete Publish 97 ability-system port. It implements the published attribute weights and group limits, 1,501-point normal stages (2,381/2,556 for first stages of one-slot pets capped at three/two slots), and species slot limits for supported common pets. Custom pets can reach five slots. Advanced magic schools, special moves, area-effect purchases, regen purchases, planning queues and daily training-hour bonuses are not included. Existing innate pet abilities continue to work. Combat pacing is shard-specific: 10,000 qualifying damage per stage, capped at 50% per target below three slots and 25% thereafter; targets more than 50 Wrestling points below the pet do not contribute. Owner must be alive and nearby.

Healing costs 100 training points and starts Healing/Anatomy at a minimum of 20; these skills improve through use. Learned healing uses native pet AI, heals self and owner within 12 tiles with line of sight, and uses two-second bandages without consumables. A matching power scroll in your backpack raises the chosen skill cap without granting current skill points. Completing a purchased five-slot stage gives the character a permanent follower-capacity floor of six, the requested +1 over the normal five; the reward never stacks. Already-higher capacities are preserved.

Use [tithe 1000 to convert 1,000 wallet gold into Chivalry tithing points, or enter an amount in [wallet and choose Tithe gold for Chivalry. One gold buys one point. The normal 100,000-point limit applies; only the amount that fits is charged. Your wallet stays on double-click gold pickup.

References for the training model: https://uo.com/wiki/ultima-online-wiki/skills/animal-taming/animal-training/ and the ServUO pub57 Pet Training implementation. No later-era spell or mastery ability is advertised unless implemented in this shard.

Stable fees use wallet gold first, then loose backpack gold and bank funds. The existing 30-gold charge is unchanged. Hidden pet training and rarity records do not count as cargo; actual items must still be unloaded from pack pets before stabling. This build does not charge a separate pet retrieval fee.

Sending a companion on a mission closes its control menu. A successful return reopens Orders for an online owner, including automatic timed returns and Return now. Failed dispatches keep Tasks open, and [c remains available to check an active trip.

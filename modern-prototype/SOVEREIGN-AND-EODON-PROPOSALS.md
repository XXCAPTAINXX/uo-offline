# Sovereign rewards and Eodon pet proposals

The lantern is implemented; the remaining entries are proposals.

- **Prospector's Lantern:** implemented for 100 Sovereigns in UO Store / Misc. Grants an account-wide 25% relative increase to custom legendary world-drop chance for one real-time hour, retaining eligibility checks. Does not stack. [fortune reports remaining time; no buff-bar icon is included.
- **Artificer's Lens:** improves the attribute intensity of a successful random equipment drop rather than creating extra drops. Current custom legendary equipment already rolls at 90–100 intensity, so a useful implementation should target ordinary generated equipment or offer a clearly bounded reroll; it must not promise a meaningful boost to an already-maxed range.
- **Dungeon Bounty Writ:** choose a dungeon, complete a fixed number of qualifying boss kills, then select a themed weapon or crafting reward. Persistent progress would make it useful even during unlucky sessions.

For Eodon, a triceratops would suit a durable physical tank with a short charge/knockback, while a saber-toothed tiger would suit a fast attacker with a pounce and bleed. Both native creature bodies exist. Their proper mounted artwork has not been verified, so rideability remains an investigation rather than a completed feature. Avoid substituting an unrelated mount appearance without discussing it.

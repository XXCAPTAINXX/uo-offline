# Champion power scroll rewards

Increase Config/Champions.cfg PowerScrolls from 6 to 12. Eligible solo players receive all 12 directly in their backpack; multiple eligible players share the base pool. Existing Felucca, range, damage-rights, dead-player corpse delivery and Justice rules remain in force. Justice protection can add copies beyond the base pool. The native system uses this same setting for mastery primer count.

ChampionScrollSmoke exercises native Barracoon reward delivery from direct player damage, an owned pet, and a bound Haven companion. Each must award the configured count to the player's backpack. The initial test fixture lacked Player=true and was corrected before accepting the results.

The reported missing reward was not reproduced with valid player fixtures. Do not claim that changing the count fixes every missing-reward cause or that the reported kill was reimbursed.

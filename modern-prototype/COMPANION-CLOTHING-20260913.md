# Companion clothing progression

Worn companion clothing now joins the existing 20-level equipment XP system. Startup, wardrobe restoration and credited kills attach missing records; existing records keep their XP. Appearance, dye hue and paperdoll removal remain unchanged. This includes replacement clothes equipped on the companion, not just the fallback outfit.

Per garment, level 1 to 20:

- Spell damage: 10 to 25 percent
- Health: 5 to 10; mana: 8 to 20
- Health regeneration: 1 to 3; mana regeneration: 2 to 4
- Lower mana cost: 3 to 5 percent
- Weapon damage: 5 to 10 percent

Higher existing item attributes are preserved. Native equipment totals and caps apply. Wildfire reads the same native SpellDamage total verified by the test. XP comes from the existing companion credited-hostile-kill route while equipped; 1,900 XP reaches level 20.

CompanionClothingSmoke checks progression, level-20 values, repeated application, preserved XP/art/hue, paperdoll movability, and native equipped versus backpack spell-damage totals.

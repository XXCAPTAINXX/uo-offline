# Integrated cove shelter wall

HavenCoveShelterWall moves the existing five stone wall pieces to the notice shelter's southern roof edge, replacing its two wooden supports on that edge. The board, flags and lamps move with the wall. The roof and opposite wooden wall remain intact, with side access to the shelter.

Coordinates derive from the existing managed roof tiles. The migration checks fixture counts, occupancy and native walking paths to the board, shelter interior and junction. Failure restores original item locations. A saved fixture marker prevents repeated movement, preserving later user decoration.

The isolated saved-world migration passed all three routes and repeat-application checks. No client-side map/art update is required. In-game visual inspection was not performed during this change.

# Mini champion expeditions

Use [minichamp, choose Travel to camp while out of combat, then start a theme near camp. [haventest page4 includes travel and feedback entries. [haven also links the encounter.

- Themes: Wildwood uprising, Corsair raiders, Restless dead.
- Three waves of five enemies and one boss. Starter encounter, one shared camp; 20-minute time limit, two-minute cooldown.
- Damage from your companion or controlled pet credits you. Every damage participant receives completion rewards, even if another participant lands the killing blow. Nearby spectators do not qualify.
- Each participant receives20Marks, a10,000gold check and250resources in a ledger-compatible deed: Yew logs, Iron ingots or Bones by theme.15% chance of an Alacrity or0.5Transcendence scroll for Lumberjacking/Tactics/Resisting Spells respectively.
- Items arrive directly in your backpack. A full pack preserves the complete item reward internally; use Collect pending rewards after making room. Marks are credited separately. No reward bag is deposited.
- Wins, active waves, participation and pending rewards persist through saves/restarts. Reward collection is owner-only and cannot replay.
- Enemies return within20tiles of camp. If all living connected players leave32tiles for two minutes, or20minutes expire, the encounter disperses without completion rewards. Deleted enemies abort rather than count as kills.
- Ordinary corpses decay after45seconds; the boss uses the native corpse duration.

This is the first modern-preview mini champion. It does not port the older island mini spawn or its complete reward catalog. Combat balance and graphical usability need live player feedback.

# Haven free skills

Player budget: 1,200 counted skill points and 300 combined stats. These 25 skills do not consume the total skill budget. They still require training and retain their individual caps and powerscroll requirements. Use [skillbudget for the complete list and your counted total.

Alchemy, Animal Lore, Animal Taming, Arms Lore, Begging, Camping, Cartography, Cooking, Detecting Hidden, Fishing, Bowcraft/Fletching, Focus, Forensic Evaluation, Herding, Hiding, Inscription, Item Identification, Lockpicking, Lumberjacking, Mining, Musicianship, Remove Trap, Snooping, Taste Identification, Tracking.

The list combines InsaneUO's 21 listed free skills, UOAlive's additional Bowcraft/Fletching, and Haven's previously free Animal Lore, Animal Taming and Focus (Snooping overlaps). Skill-effect changes from those servers are not imported by this budget change.

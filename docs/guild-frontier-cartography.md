# Guild creation, frontier rewards and house cartography

- Guild creation now accepts spaces and punctuation while retaining the profanity filter. Invalid wording and a name already in use have separate messages. Use **Rare Export Company** with abbreviation **REC**: the normal abbreviation limit is four characters, so `R.E.C.` is too long.
- **[expeditions** opens a readable reward catalog. Select a currency to inspect the exact starting properties and evolution before confirming a purchase. No currency is charged for previews.
- The Saltfang's cargo seal now spawns aft on the starboard side, clear of its old position under the sail, and announces when the crew's protection drops.
- The upstairs R.E.C. cartography area has a working **Treasure-map travel library** and **Cartographer's treasure-map chest**. They can also be bought from **Arcane Supplies** for **15,000** and **5,000 gold**, respectively. Place them in your house and secure/lock them down normally.
- The library browses the current Trammel/Felucca treasure catalog. **Find the rune for my decoded map** targets a decoded, unfinished map in your pack and shows its facet and coordinates before travel. This matching path accepts maps on other facets, including Malas and Ter Mur, when those maps exist; it does not invent new map loot or treasure sites. Normal travel restrictions apply, and you still dig and defeat guardians normally.
- The map chest stores treasure maps, SOS messages and message bottles. Its search can find facet, level, or treasure coordinates. **Collect item / bag** checks nested bags and leaves unrelated goods. The house receiving chest also includes secured map-chest contents, so you can withdraw maps from the master point.

## In-game checks

1. Create the guild through the normal Guild menu using Rare Export Company / REC.
2. Open [expeditions, inspect each reward and currency, then confirm a purchase only when desired.
3. Start a Corsair patrol and locate the seal on the aft starboard deck. It remains protected until the crew and captain are defeated.
4. Upstairs in the house, open the bookshelf library and target your decoded map. Confirm the displayed destination, travel, and dig normally.
5. Target a mixed bag with the map chest's Collect action. Confirm only supported maps/SOS/bottles move, and retrieve a map through the receiving chest.

# Wraith Form and trial resource deeds

Caster companions automatically cast native Wraith Form when they have 20 Necromancy and 17 mana. Emergency cures and healing take priority. An active transformation is not repeatedly toggled; Wraith Form is cleared when leaving caster role or on death/deletion. Failed attempts are throttled to ten seconds. Native Spirit Speak scaling and the victim's available mana govern spell-damage mana leech.

New island-trial wave drops and personal completion rewards package crafting materials in filled commodity deeds weighing one stone each. Earth waves award iron ingots because native raw ore is not deedable. Other resource types and quantities are unchanged. Redeem a deed by putting it in your bank and double-clicking it, or using a secured commodity deed box.

# Pirate headquarters wooden ladders

The eight thin rope nets at the R.E.C. headquarters now use broad wooden ladder artwork with solid wooden steps at their feet. Double-click either the ladder or its steps to travel directly to the connected floor. There is no destination menu.

The update keeps each existing ladder's serial, location, route, and clear approach. The attached steps occupy the same tile and delegate to the ladder's existing house permissions, distance, line-of-sight, and landing checks. Standing on a rung is allowed; using a ladder through the next floor is rejected. Reapplying the headquarters update reuses the same eight steps.

The house verification exercises all eight routes from both click targets, checks the front approaches and line of sight, and retains the storage, crafting-station, and floor-access checks. In game, try the wooden step at the bottom of a ladder if furniture or your character overlaps its upper artwork.

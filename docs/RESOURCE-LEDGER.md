# Resource Ledger and varied gathering missions

Buy the blessed, one-stone Resource Ledger from Arcane Supplies for 1,000 gold (wallet accepted). Keep it in your backpack. Drop filled commodity deeds onto it, target a deed, or use Absorb pack deeds to gather supported deeds from nested backpack containers. Matching materials combine into persistent balances. Enter 1–60,000 and select a resource to withdraw that amount as a new commodity deed; redeem it in a bank or secured commodity deed box. Failed/full-pack withdrawals do not reduce balances.

Supported resources: all nine ingot types; seven log and board types; regular, spined, horned and barbed leather and hides; eight magery and five necromancy reagents; arrows, bolts, feathers, bones, and undyed cloth/uncut cloth/cloth bolts. Grades and logs/boards/hides/leather remain separate. Unsupported, renamed, dyed or specially blessed materials are left untouched. Book balances survive saves and cannot be used while the book is outside your backpack.

Gathering missions keep their total quantities but split them equally between basic and an unlocked specialty when one is available. Mining unlocks dull copper65, shadow iron70, copper75, bronze80, gold85, agapite90, verite95, valorite99. Lumberjacking unlocks oak65, ash80, yew95, heartwood/bloodwood/frostwood100. Leather uses the LOWER of Wrestling and Tactics: spined65, horned80, barbed100. Selection favors the best unlocked tier, with equal-skill top woods sharing that preference; lower unlocked grades remain possible. Both manual and AFK missions use these rewards.

New companion mission rewards go directly into its pack, with no wrapping supply bags. Normal overflow handling remains; automatic missions keep earned rewards in the companion pack and pause future departures near capacity. Existing reward bags are unchanged.

Validation:166 real-map tests passed, including every catalog resource combining/partial withdrawal/native bank redemption, saved balances, ownership, overflow/full-pack rollback, skill thresholds, direct delivery and duplicate-return prevention.

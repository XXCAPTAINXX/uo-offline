# R.E.C. Corsair compound

The 31-by-31 customizable plot at 4196, 2868 Trammel is now a compound with separate captain's house, crafting lodge, and barn. The house has three usable floors. Upper wooden galleries connect the buildings, with pitched tile/slate roofs, cannons, a salvaged anchor, maritime supplies and a planted courtyard. The house sign still opens native customization.

The original receiving chest and thirteen linked profession stores retain their identities and contents. The receiving counter is in the courtyard; crafting supplies stay with their respective stations. The barn includes pet storage and shrinking facilities. Ship's stairs connect the three floors. The charter is in the guild council room. Guild creation uses the character Guild menu.

The home mini champion remains on the north shore, behind the compound at 4198, 2836.

## Home patrol

Use [home and walk a few paces west/northwest of the landing to the supported noticeboard at 4205, 2924. It has a timber shelter, mounting post and lantern. Double-click for Start/join patrol, turn in backpack maritime cargo, or the expedition reward journal. The board connects to the existing shared Corsair encounter, preserving its participants, cooldown and rewards. It does not create an extra encounter farm.

The Saltfang now uses the larger Britannian hull, with boarding and enemy spawns on its correct deck height. [voyage exit returns to Chelonia and [home returns to your island.

## Companion orders

Say all guard me, all follow me, all stay, all stop, all come, all kill or all attack. The companion also recognizes its full name or first name followed by these orders. Owner and distance checks still apply. Plain speech works even when the client omits speech keywords. All kill/attack uses the standard shared pet targeting cursor.

Wild custom tameables are excluded from automatic companion aggression and harmful area effects. The owner must explicitly target one through Attack or a spoken kill/attack command. Authorization applies only to that animal and ends when a different standing order is issued; it does not persist through restart. Taming-assist peacemaking and healing remain available.

## Weapon mana sustain

Starter weapons begin with 20% Hit Mana Leech. Evolution raises the minimum to 36% at level 5, 57% at level 10, 78% at level 15, and 100% at level 20. Evolving boss, Legendary, quest and companion weapons use the same minimum progression while preserving stronger existing rolls. Ordinary equipment does not gain evolution or automatic leech. Existing starter weapons update on load; existing special weapon progression updates after load. Starter grimoires gain mana regeneration from 1 up to 7 instead of a weapon-hit property that would not trigger on spells.

100% is the chance to trigger Hit Mana Leech on a successful hit, not a promise to refill the mana bar: native leech converts 40% of damage into mana.

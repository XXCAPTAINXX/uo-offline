# Companion hunting support

Companions automatically skin the hunting group's nearby kills between fights,
collect hides, and cut them into leather in their shared pack. This is enabled
by default; toggle **Hunting: skin + cut** under **Companion > Tasks**. The
preference is saved on the owner's account.

The companion searches within six tiles while following or guarding, stays
within twelve tiles of the owner, and needs line of sight. Combat, healing,
poison, taming assistance, stay orders and expeditions take priority. Only
animal/monster corpses with hides and the group's contribution qualify; player,
human, pet, summoned and animated corpses are excluded. The owner's normal loot
rights must permit harvesting.

Standard creature carving and scissors determine quantities and leather types.
Meat and other loot stay on the corpse. A full companion pack leaves materials
on the corpse. Repeated visits cannot carve the same corpse twice. Field tools
are built in; the owner does not need to equip a knife or supply scissors.

**Companion > Stats** now opens an expanded three-column view of every skill,
sorted alphabetically, with trained base, effective value and cap. Core stats,
resistances and the hunting toggle's current status are also shown. This is a
display change; skinning does not invent a separate skill or add skill gains.

Install the prebuilt update only after saving and shutting down the server.
The updater backs up the previous assembly, sources and saves. Existing
companions and their saved progress are retained.

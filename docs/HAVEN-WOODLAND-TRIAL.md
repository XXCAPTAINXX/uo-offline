# Haven woodland trial and evolving rewards

Double-click the woodland trial stone near 3474,2723 on Trammel's New Haven island. Three waves of six weak creatures (at most three alive), then Briarheart (450 HP, 3–6 damage). The champion drops 2,000–3,000 gold, five random 105/110 non-crafting power scrolls and 20 Haven marks. Nearby players with standard boss damage rights receive five Astral shards in their backpacks (at their feet if full). Pets credit their owners normally. Two-minute restart cooldown; abandoned events reset after 15 minutes without kills. Progress and creature references persist across saves; deleted mobs are replaced without kill credit.

Astral purchases evolve to level 20. The weaver ring gains spell damage, mana regeneration, lower mana cost and cast recovery; the mantle gains health, defense and regeneration; earrings gain luck, regeneration and both damage types. Existing pieces update on their next shared combat XP gain, including capped pieces. Level 1 appears before the first gain.

Named Haven quest equipment now evolves, and future necromancy quest rewards use an evolving book. Plain previously awarded necromancy books cannot be distinguished from ordinary books. Tools and consumables do not become evolving armor. Haven quest armor covers head, chest, arms, hands and legs, but not the neck slot.

Use [HavenRewards or the starter supply stone's Claim earned Haven training rewards entry. Each of the 26 skill-training quests is eligible when its base-skill threshold is met. Completed quests cannot pay twice; active quests must be turned in normally. A full backpack does not consume the claim. Uses persistent native quest completion records.

Finishing pet training no longer increases player follower capacity. A level-20 Haven talisman of concord unlocks permanent capacity 6 once, through shared XP while worn or double-clicking it in your inventory. Existing higher capacity is preserved; old untracked capacity awards are not removed.

Custom pet mission odds: Common 40%, Rare 35%, Epic 20%, Legendary 5%. Ordinary species remain unmodified.

# Frostbound bear and pet dyes

Staged only. No live spawn or server update has been applied.

The Frostbound bear is a real tameable mount using the client's existing polar-bear body 0xD5 and mounted art 0x3EC5. Its den is at **942, 116, ground height, Tokuno**, in the northern snowfields. An administrator can install it with **[HavenSnowSetup** after deployment is authorized. Setup is idempotent. The Wayfarer's Atlas adds the den under Tokuno towns once installed.

One bear roams a connected area within twelve tiles. A replacement appears five minutes after taming or death. Tamed or previously owned bears are never removed by the den's cleanup or recalled to it. Initial rarity is 50% Rare, 35% Epic, 15% Legendary; taming requirements are 100, 110 and 120 respectively. Rare/Epic use three follower slots; Legendary starts at one, following the user's existing custom-pet rule.

Before rarity bonuses, the bear has 450–550 Strength, 180–210 Dexterity/stamina, 100–150 Intelligence, 600–750 health and 17–23 base damage, split equally physical/cold. Native taming stat loss is disabled. Rarity adds the shard's normal custom-pet bonuses. Animal Lore, pet training, learned Healing, shrinking and the optional custom-pet pack remain available. Its trained elemental pool uses the frost theme.

**Colossal Rage:** landing melee attacks below half health starts a ten-second frenzy for +50% melee damage, with a thirty-second cooldown. A roar and effect announce it. It is innate and does not consume a training choice. The effect is Haven's adaptation; another server's private damage formula has not been claimed or copied.

The pet-identity pass adds Guardian Roar, thick-hide resistance floors and four rarity coat shades. See PET-SIGNATURES.md. Original-color restoration now restores the species' rarity shade for custom pets; chosen dye overrides remain intact.

**Pet dye:** Training Supplies → Pet supplies and mastery, 5,000 wallet-compatible gold. Each bottle offers eight swatches or original-color restoration. Select a color, then target your own living pet or its shrunken token. Dismount before applying. A successful application consumes the bottle; invalid targets, unchanged colors and cancellation preserve it. It does not change rarity, stats, species or abilities. Original color persists with the pet and can be restored after shrinking or ownership transfer.

Research: UOAlive's [rideable polar bear page](https://uoalive.com/wiki/Rideable_Polar_Bear) identifies northern Tokuno and Colossal Rage. Its [pet dye guide](https://uoalive.com/wiki/Pet_Dyes) documents cosmetic pet coloring. Haven uses its own prices, palette and rules. Exact UOAlive spawn coordinates were not published in the referenced text; the Haven clearing was verified against this server's terrain.

Validation: real mount art/ownership, Rage damage/cooldown, snow terrain, spawn deduplication, preservation of released pets, dye ownership, no stat changes and original-color restoration after shrinking passed. Live client appearance still requires an in-game review after deployment is authorized.

# Companion taming and assigned pets

The Bard companion's **Tame assist** now calms the selected wild animal, approaches it, and uses the normal Animal Taming attempt. It continues healing its owner. Taming and Animal Lore requirements, follower capacity, subduing, ownership limits, and native taming chances still apply. Stop assist cancels the attempt; leaving, death, or changing role also ends it.

A successful tame becomes a **Pet claim** in the companion backpack. This stores the actual animal, preserving its species, rarity, skills, stats, and training after normal taming adjustments. Move the claim into your own backpack and double-click it to release the pet; normal follower capacity applies. It is ready to bond when fed suitable food if you meet its taming requirement. Animal Lore can inspect the claim. If the companion pack is full, the animal remains assigned to the companion until you make room and reclaim it.

Use **[companionpets** to assign, manage, or reclaim pets. You can also target the companion with your pet's normal **Transfer** command. Both pet and owner must stand within three tiles of the companion, outside combat. The companion needs sufficient Taming, Lore, and follower capacity. Dead, summoned, humanoid, and currently ridden pets cannot be assigned.

Mounts are ridden by default. Turn **Ride off** to have a mount follow and fight instead. Other assigned animals follow the companion and attack its combat target. They refrain from attacking during taming assistance. Assigned pets wait safely during missions and return with the companion. **Reclaim** places the original pet in a claim ticket in the companion pack; this does not reroll rarity or create a new animal.

## In-game checks

1. Select Bard, choose Tame assist, and target an ordinary wild horse within reach. The companion should calm it and begin taming. The successful claim should appear in its pack. Repeat with Stop assist to check cancellation.
2. Lore a claim, then move it into your pack and release it. Confirm the same stats and rarity, and feed it to bond.
3. Transfer a pet horse to the companion. It should mount; use [companionpets and turn Ride off to make it follow and fight.
4. Send a mission with an assigned walking pet; confirm it returns. Reclaim it through [companionpets.

Validation includes the normal native taming-start path, native pet-transfer dispatch, original-animal claims, owner restrictions, follower limits, dismounting, mission parking/return, full-pack recovery, and assignment serialization.

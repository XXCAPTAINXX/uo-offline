# Haven Mark Exchange

Open `[wallet` and select **Haven rewards**, or use a Special Reward Stone. The exchange contains 54 offers in nine categories, including 34 additions. Select an item to see its price, description and equipment properties before buying.

Purchases use carried wallet marks first, then loose marks in your backpack and its bags. Existing bracelet prices remain 15 marks, with the existing 25,000-gold alternative if you lack marks. Every other offer requires marks. Insufficient funds or insufficient backpack space leave your currency untouched.

## New equipment

These four blessed pieces gain the existing shared combat experience while equipped, up to level 20. Their fixed skill bonuses apply while worn; leveling adds Luck and Strength, Dexterity and Intelligence. The sash and doublet occupy the same equipment slot.

| Item | Marks | Starting bonuses |
|---|---:|---|
| Bulwark boots | 180 | +10 Parrying, +5 Healing, +5% defense chance, +5 Strength, +2 health regeneration |
| Tideweaver sash | 200 | +10 Spellweaving, +10 Focus, +5% lower mana cost, +5% spell damage, +2 mana regeneration |
| Beastkeeper's doublet | 220 | +10 Animal Taming, Animal Lore and Veterinary; +5% defense chance, +2 health regeneration |
| Prospector's apron | 140 | +10 Mining, Lumberjacking and Fishing; +150 Luck, +5 Strength, +2 stamina regeneration |

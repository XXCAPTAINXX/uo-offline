# Legendary pet over-cap skills

Legendary CUSTOM pets have a 50% one-time chance to roll 1–3 already-active trainable skills at 125–150. Each selected skill's cap rises to at least its rolled value. Unused skills are not invented and existing higher values are never reduced. Ordinary pets and lower rarities do not qualify. The existing one-slot Legendary starting benefit remains.

New pet tickets roll before their first preview and keep the same actual pet through claiming. Previously reserved Legendary tickets receive their one-time roll when inspected. Existing owned Legendary custom pets roll on their next Animal Lore overview or controlled combat attack. Both successful and unsuccessful rolls are stored in a new virtual marker, so inspecting, refreshing, reclaiming, saving or restarting cannot reroll them. Animal Lore shows actual skill/cap values and pet properties list the selected Legendary skills.

Validation:169 local real-map tests pass, including success, failure at the 50% boundary, no repeated rolls, non-Legendary exclusion, serialized selection, and preview/claim preservation.

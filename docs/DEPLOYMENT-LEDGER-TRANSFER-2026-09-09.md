# Ledger transfers and book inventory slots — deployed

Open a Resource Ledger, choose **Transfer all...**, and target a different ledger in your backpack. All stored balances merge into the destination; the source stays available with empty balances. This supports a nearby owned companion's ledger or another ledger in your backpack. Ownership, possession and range are rechecked at target selection. Every resource is validated before either book changes; overflow rejects the whole transfer. Transfers need no intermediate deeds or extra inventory slots and do not duplicate mission receipts.

The Champion's Codex now occupies one backpack slot regardless of archived scrolls or other accepted items. Its contents no longer propagate inventory counts to parent bags or the player. Existing books receive corrected counts during the server's normal world-load totals rebuild; serialization is unchanged. Default codices have no internal item-count limit; explicit administrator limits remain honored. Weight checks remain active. Combining and splitting scrolls works with a full backpack, while extracting a scroll still needs room for that item. The Resource Ledger already stores numeric balances rather than retained deeds and likewise uses one slot.
